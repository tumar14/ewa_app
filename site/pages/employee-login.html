Employee login

Sign in to see your earned wages

Check what you have earned, what you can access before payday, and your past advances.

Continue to sign in

Use the email address and password you set when you claimed your account.

Sign in as an employee

Forgot your password? Choose “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen.

First time here?

You need to claim your account once:

  1. Get your code

    Ask your HR administrator for your one-time code.

  2. Choose “Claim your account”

    It is under the sign-in button. Pick your company.

  3. Enter your details

    Your employee number, your name as HR has it, and the code. Then set your own password.

Nobody from Hola Manje will ever ask for your password. Looking for the employer sign-in? Employer login
