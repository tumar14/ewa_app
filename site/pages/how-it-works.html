How it works

From earned to paid, in plain steps

Hola Manje gives employees access to wages they have already earned. It gives employers the controls, records and reconciliation they need to run it safely.

For employees

Getting an advance

  1. Your hours are recorded

    Your employer records the hours you work. Hola Manje turns them into the wages you have earned so far this pay cycle.

  2. See what you can access

    The app shows your earned wages, the most you can access right now, any fee, and your next payday.

  3. Request an amount

    Pick an amount within your limit and review the fee. Confirm only if you are happy with the cost.

  4. Your employer approves

    Your HR or admin team reviews the request. You can follow its progress in the app.

  5. You are paid

    Your employer's finance team sends the money by bank transfer, mobile money or cash, and marks it as paid.

  6. Repaid on payday

    The advance and fee come off your next pay automatically.

For employers

Running it safely

  1. Register your company

    Send a registration request. Our team reviews it and creates your company and first administrator.

  2. Add your employees

    Load your staff list. Each employee claims their own account with their employee number, name and a one-time code from HR.

  3. Set your policy

    Choose the percentage of earned wages employees can access, the fee, how many advances per cycle, and your payday schedule.

  4. Approve and pay

    Review requests, pay approved advances, and record each payment.

  5. Run payroll deductions

    At the end of the cycle, Hola Manje totals what to deduct from each employee.

  6. Settle and reconcile

    Confirm what payroll actually deducted, record the settlement, and resolve any difference with a written note.

Where the money goes

Hola Manje is the system of record: it calculates what employees may access, tracks every request, and reconciles payroll deductions and employer settlements.

Hola Manje does not hold or send your money. Today the employer's finance team pays each approved advance and records it in the platform. Payment connections to banks and mobile money providers are not live, and nothing in the app is presented as a payment unless a person has made and recorded it.

Settlement works the same way. Each pay cycle produces a settlement with a unique reference. The employer pays against that reference and the payment is checked against the bank statement before it is recorded.
