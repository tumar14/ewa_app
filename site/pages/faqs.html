FAQs

Questions, answered plainly

Cannot find your answer? Ask us.

For employees

What is Hola Manje?

Hola Manje lets you access part of the wages you have already earned before payday. Your employer sets the rules and approves each request, and the amount is deducted from your next pay.

How much can I access?

Your employer sets a maximum share of your earned wages for each pay cycle, and a maximum number of advances per cycle. The app shows your limit right now, and it updates as you earn.

Is there a fee?

That depends on your employer's settings. If there is a fee, the app shows the exact amount before you confirm, and the fee is fixed when you request. It does not grow if you are repaid later.

How do I get the money?

After your employer approves the request, their finance team pays you by bank transfer, mobile money or cash and marks it as paid in the app. Timing depends on your employer's payment process. Hola Manje itself does not send the money.

When is it paid back?

On payday. The advance and the fee are deducted from your pay.

What if my pay does not cover what I took?

The shortfall is flagged to your employer's HR and finance team, who decide how to handle it under your employer's policy. Ask your HR administrator for your employer's rules before you request large amounts.

How do I sign in for the first time?

Ask HR for your one-time code. On the sign-in page choose “Claim your account”, pick your company, and enter your employee number, your name and the code. Then set your own password.

I forgot my password.

Use “Forgot password?” on the sign-in page. If that does not work, contact your HR administrator.

For employers

How do I register my company?

Use the registration form. Our team reviews each request, contacts you, and then creates your company and first administrator. The form itself never creates an account.

What does it cost?

Pricing is agreed with each employer. Tell us about your company on the registration or contact form.

Who decides the limits and fees?

You do. You set the percentage of earned wages, the fee, the advances per cycle and your payday schedule. They are enforced on our servers.

Do we have to pay out the advances?

Today, yes. Your finance team pays each approved advance by your usual method and records it in Hola Manje. Direct payment connections are not live.

How do payroll deductions and settlements work?

For each pay cycle, Hola Manje totals what to deduct from each employee and creates a settlement with a payment reference. You confirm what payroll actually deducted, pay against the reference, and any difference stays open as an exception until someone resolves it with a note.

Can other companies, or Hola Manje, see our staff and payroll?

No. Each company's data is separated. Hola Manje platform administrators can register and suspend companies but cannot browse your employees, advances or payroll.

Does it connect to our payroll software?

Not automatically. Deduction totals are produced for your payroll team to apply. Ask us if you need a specific integration.

Security and data

Is my information safe?

Read the security and privacy page for the details: role-based access, separated company data, server-side checks and an audit log.

Do you sell my data?

No. Data is used only to run the service and is shared only where Eswatini law requires.
