Employer registration

Register your company

Tell us about your business. We review every request and contact you before anything is set up.

Please do not include passwords, bank details or pay figures.

What happens next

  1. We review

    A member of our team reads your request.

  2. We contact you

    We confirm details, answer questions and agree terms with you.

  3. We set up your company

    We create your company and one administrator. They sign in with a temporary password and must change it on first use.

This form does not create an account, and nobody is given administrator access until a person has reviewed your request.
