How it works

Four steps from earned to paid

  1. You work and earn

    Hours recorded by your employer build up the wages you have earned this pay cycle.

  2. You request an advance

    Choose an amount within your limit. The fee, if any, is shown before you confirm.

  3. Your employer approves and pays

    Your employer reviews the request. Their finance team sends the money and records the payment.

  4. Repaid on payday

    The advance and fee are deducted from your pay. Your employer settles the total with Hola Manje's records.

Trust by design

Limits that are enforced, records that are kept

Server-side limits

Advance limits are checked on our servers, not just in the app, so they cannot be bypassed from a phone.

Separated company data

Each employer sees only its own people and payroll. Hola Manje staff who register companies cannot browse your employees or advances.

An audit trail

Requests, approvals, payments, payroll runs and settlements are logged with who did what and when.

Security and privacy

Ready to offer earned wage access? Tell us about your company. Our team reviews every registration and sets up your account. Nobody is given administrator access automatically.
