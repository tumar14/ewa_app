For employers, HR and finance

Offer earned wage access without losing control

You set the rules, you approve every advance, and every deduction and settlement is tracked down to the cent.

Register your company Employer login

What you can do

1

Register your company

Send a registration request. Our team reviews it and sets up your company and first administrator. Nobody is granted administrator access automatically.

2

Manage employees

Load your staff list and issue one-time codes. Employees claim their own accounts. Deactivate someone and their sign-in is disabled.

3

Configure your policy

Set the maximum share of earned wages, the service fee, the number of advances per cycle and your payday schedule. Limits are enforced on our servers.

4

View transactions

See every advance with its status, amount and fee. Filter by pending, to be paid, paid or declined, and copy the list into a spreadsheet.

5

Review payroll deductions

Run payroll for a cycle and see what to deduct from each employee. Anyone whose pay does not cover their advances is flagged, never silently ignored.

6

Reconcile settlements

Each cycle gets a settlement with a payment reference. Confirm what payroll deducted, record payments, and resolve any difference with a written note.

Controls your finance team will look for

  • Role-based access, with permissions you can adjust for supervisors
  • An audit log of requests, approvals, payments, payroll runs and settlements
  • Amounts held in whole cents, so totals always add up
  • A settlement is never marked settled while a difference is unresolved
  • Duplicate bank payments are counted once
  • Your data is separated from every other company's

A worked example

Illustrative figures only.

Payroll should deduct E 50,000.00 for the cycle. Payroll confirms it deducted E 49,500.00 and the employer pays that amount.
