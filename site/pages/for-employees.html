For employees

Your earned wages, in your hands

Check what you have earned, see what you can access before payday, and know the cost before you confirm anything.

Employee login How it works

Everything you need on one screen

E

What you have earned

Your wages for this pay cycle, built up from the hours your employer records.

%

What you can access

Your limit right now, based on your earnings and the rules your employer has set.

F

Fees, clearly shown

If your employer charges a fee, you see the exact amount, and what will be deducted on payday, before you confirm.

H

Your past advances

A history of every advance: when you asked, whether it was approved, and whether it has been paid.

P

Your next payday

How many days until you are paid, and where you are in the pay cycle.

M

Made for phones

Use it in your phone's browser or the app. It is designed for small screens and quick checks.

Your first time

  1. Ask HR for your code

    Your employer gives you a one-time code. You will also need your employee number and your name as HR has it.

  2. Claim your account

    Open the sign-in page and choose “Claim your account”. Pick your company, enter your details and set your own password.

  3. Read and accept the terms

    You will see how your data is used and how advances work before you start.

Good to know

  • An advance is part of wages you have already earned, not extra pay.
  • It is deducted from your next pay, with the fee shown up front.
  • Your employer sets the limit and approves each request.
  • Nobody from Hola Manje will ask for your password. Never share it.
