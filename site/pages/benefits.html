Benefits

Good for the people who work, good for the business that pays them

Earned wage access works when it is clear, controlled and well recorded. That is what Hola Manje is built around.

Employees

More control between paydays

  • Help with real-life timing. School fees, transport and emergencies do not always wait for the end of the month.
  • Fewer reasons to borrow elsewhere. Access to your own earned wages can reduce the need for short-term borrowing.
  • No guesswork. Your limit, the fee and the deduction are shown before you confirm.
  • A record you can read. Every advance and its status are in your history.
  • Simple to use. Built for phones, with plain language and large buttons.

Employers

A benefit you can actually govern

  • You stay in control. You choose the limits and approve each request.
  • A benefit employees value. Many employees appreciate flexible access to wages they have earned.
  • Clean payroll handling. Deductions are totalled for each employee and each cycle.
  • Reconciliation built in. Expected, confirmed and received amounts are tracked, and differences are flagged until resolved.
  • An audit trail. Know who requested, approved and paid, and when.

A note on expectations. Hola Manje does not promise particular results for retention, productivity or employee finances. Outcomes depend on how an employer sets its policy and how employees use it. We would rather show you the controls than make claims we cannot back up.
